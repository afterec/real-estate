02 · AFTEREC
Who we are

We don't make “a video”.
We build the image of a property.

AfteREC is a film production company. We produce content that holds the viewer's attention: through narrative, shot selection, lighting and set decoration. For each property, we show the best the house has, its uniqueness, in a visual piece that makes people want to know more. We seek to work with unique, exclusive properties, mainly in the luxury sector, and this content strengthens the personal brand of the agent and of the company associated with the sale of the property.

We coordinate the best professionals for each piece of content and guarantee that everything we produce maintains a high level of quality. What you can expect is not just the delivery of an asset: it is the management of all the marketing content of a property, a development or a project, especially cinematic, carefully crafted content.

Why this matters

In luxury, the problem is not exposure. It is sustaining value.

Today anyone can make a video, but few create content with a cinematic language that also works online. That is what changes the positioning of the property and of those who sell it: it speeds up the sale, reduces curious visitors, improves lead quality and makes the buyer arrive at the viewing already understanding the house. And we guarantee all of this happens.

The better and more appealing the content, the more enquiries it generates and the less time the property stays on the market. And less time on the market means lower costs: maintaining a luxury property can cost 5 to 10 thousand euros per month, between gardens, electricity, water and the essentials to keep it impeccable. Selling faster means passing those costs on to the buyer sooner.

Architectural photography

From 15 to more than 35 images captured and edited by photographers specialised in architecture. 07 · VIDEO
← All content
Cinematic video

It's not an ad. It's a film.

A longer video, produced to the standards of cinema: a crew with cinematography, lighting and art direction, and post-production with colour grading. The script is the fundamental piece: it is the narrative that holds the viewer, and it can be serious, comedic or convey a lifestyle the buyer sees themselves in and that makes them want the house. It is the piece that creates emotion and presents the property as a premium, unique product.

Ideal for truly unique properties and projects, which deserve to reach a universal audience and attract it at first sight. And it says a lot about who commissions it: an agency or brand that presents a video like this shows it cares about aesthetics and narrative, and showcases its portfolio at the highest level.

08 · VIDEO TOUR
← All content
Guided video tour

For those who want to know the property in depth.

A long format, which can reach 30 minutes depending on the size of the property, dedicated to YouTube. It walks through the house room by room and tells its story to those who truly want to know the property before deciding on an investment of this scale.

It is a video built for organic reach: on average, a video like this sits around half a million views, and truly exceptional properties reach tens of millions of views, depending on the property's exclusivity.

The format has been strongly explored in the United States over the last five years, with results in plain sight in views and in sales closed through this content. In Portugal almost no company does it, and that is also why we stand out by bringing it here.

09 · SOCIAL MEDIA
← All content
Social media content

From the air, and over time.

Short videos with dynamic, movement-driven edits, and photographs of the property, made to hold attention and generate more reach. The FPV drone aerial capture flies through every room in one continuous, cinematic trajectory, shows the relationship between the house, the land and the surroundings, and reveals the property's full potential in the fastest way, exactly what the social media viewer is looking for: understanding the house in seconds and staying hooked on the video from the first moment.

For properties under construction, a monthly time-lapse documents the build from day one to delivery: ideal for developments and developers. And when the project justifies it, we create content with artificial intelligence, which we also use in the editing of some social media content.

10 · 3D
← All content
Renders and 3D content

The property, before it exists.

Renders and 3D video for properties that do not exist yet: plots with an approved project, off-plan developments or builds still to be finished. They show the full potential of the space with quality close to the final result and make it possible to sell off-plan, long before the build is ready.

Excellent for developers, agents and landowners who need to communicate what still only exists on paper.

11 · DIGITAL
← All content
Dedicated website and 360º photography tour

One link that centralises everything.

The dedicated website is the piece that centralises the property's communication: the photographs, the cinematic video, the video tour and the 360º photography tour, in a single address and with control over who arrives.

The 360º photography tour is a piece of content in its own right: 360º photographs of each room create a virtual house the buyer can walk through remotely, room by room, on any device. It helps filter viewings: whoever takes the tour has already understood what the property is like, and whoever books a viewing after that is genuinely interested and wants to see it in person.

12 · PRINT
← All content
Printed book / catalogue

A piece that stays.

A hardcover book or catalogue, with the property's chosen photography and text. Ideal for unique houses and architectural projects with a story to tell: it shows what was designed and how it was built.

It is handed out at qualified viewings, offered to the client at closing, and it stays: an aesthetically beautiful piece that works as a decorative object, it does not disappear like a link.
